# Movie Genre Analysis

This project focuses on analyzing movie genres using the IMDb movies dataset. The analysis explores various aspects of genres, including their frequency, budget, revenue, popularity, and voting averages. The goal is to derive insights into which genres are most common, which generate the highest revenue, which are the most popular, and which have the highest number of critically acclaimed films.

## Overview
This project aims to answer several key research questions related to movie genres by analyzing the IMDb dataset. The dataset includes a variety of information about movies, such as budget, revenue, popularity, and genre. By leveraging this data, we can identify trends and correlations that are crucial for understanding the movie industry.

## Research Questions
1. **Which genres are the most common (number of movies made)?**
2. **Which genres have high average budget and revenue?**
3. **Which genres have high average popularity?**
4. **Which genres have the highest number of movies with a voting average ≥ 8?**

## Hypotheses
1. The best movies according to vote average return high profit and revenue.
2. The best movies according to popularity return high profit and revenue.
3. Highly budgeted movies return high revenue and profit.
4. Highly budgeted movies have high popularity.

## Findings
- **Most Common Genres:** Drama, Comedy, and Thriller are the most frequent genres in the dataset, with Drama being the most prevalent.
- **High Budget and Revenue:** Adventure, Action, and Fantasy genres tend to have the highest average budgets and revenues.
- **High Popularity:** Adventure, Science Fiction, and Fantasy genres have the highest average popularity scores.
- **High-Rating Movies:** Documentary, Drama, and Music genres have the highest number of movies with a voting average of 8 or higher.

The analysis supports the hypotheses that highly-rated and popular movies tend to generate higher profits and revenues, and that movies with higher budgets tend to be more popular and profitable.
